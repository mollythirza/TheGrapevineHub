Venue Lore

The Old Crown Basement

Recounted by Elena (Mutant Press) • 1:12

How a damp storage cellar underneath a local pub turned into the central pillar of the underground hardcore scene.

0:00 1:12
"It smelled permanently of stale beer and ozone, but the acoustics were surprisingly killer. Every touring band wanted to play that room because the ceiling was so low you could touch the pipes."

Why We Make Zines

Recounted by Liam (Acid Threads) • 0:45

Reflections on why physical print culture matters in an era dominated by algorithmic social media feeds.

0:00 0:45
"Algorithms decide who sees what online. When you hand someone a printed zine or a cassette tape, nobody can shadowban it. It's direct from human to human."
